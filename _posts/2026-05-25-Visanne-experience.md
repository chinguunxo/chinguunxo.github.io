This blog post is for anyone who is considering:
> - Sart taking Visanne
> - Stopping it due to side effects

## my story

I was introduced to Visanne on my first visit to an International Hospital in China. It was honestly wonderful. Compared to my previous experiences with doctors, they took my pain seriously without telling me to drink water or do exercises. Plus, I turned out to have hetergenous thickening of uterine walls, which is observed in adenomyosis.
Eventhough Visanne is a pill that is 'prescribed for endometriosis' it works for adenomyosis pain management as it stops menstrual cycle. (This varies by individual and some have spottings)

## why I stopped

The side effects started after taking the pill for 1 year for me. I started becoming incredibly unmotivated, irritated, anxious and just can't help but to sleep all the time. The turning point for me to stop the medication was when I was crying every single day, and when I noticed I was having suicidal thoughts over some stupid organic chemistry midterm result. Honestly, making the decision of stopping the pill was still not easy when all you could remember was how you were standing inside the bus in -20 degrees Celsius in pain crawling home alone. After discussing it a doctor, I stoppped the pill. I wanted to know whether I was actually having side effects or I was going mental. 

## why I went back on it

I noticed the depression leaving me within a week! I did not stress over the small things like I did for a long time and I finally felt relaxed. I actually felt happy for once! Like genuine happiness. I watched YT videos and read reddit posts of other women and they had similar mental side effects. The timeline of experiencing the side effect varried greatly from what I saw -- weeks to months to years. It really depends.
My first period came after a month of stopping the pill. PAIN PAIN PAIN. Interestingly I had to take more pain killers than what I used to to still be in pain? The discomfort continued for 4 days and I had to skip class or show up to class with my heating pad and understand nothing becuase of all the brain fog. It took me 3 weeks to get back on track with the courseload. "Atleast I am happy".
Cutting to the chase, it was just not worth constantly anticipating when the next horrible agony would suddenly start and who to go to for help when everyone around me has a different schedule and work. My last cycle, unfortunately, decided to come during my STAT final exam. The painkiller I took did not have effect and I stopped feeling my hands an hour into the exam. After the exam was over, I asked the professor whether I could retake the exam, explaining "I was in a lot of pain". He replied "Anyone can say that and retake the exam" and "No.", before running off. I was left crying, not just because I was mad at him, but because of the thought I have to live with these problems my whole adult life. I do not want to be limited by this pain.
I did not want to plan my life around my cycle because it was practically impossable for a college student studying abroad. I went back on the pill.
