At some point in your **endo-career**, you will need a way to manage sudden pain in any place. These are what I am putting in my bag:

> - ***Pain relief medications***: Personally, I use **ibuprofen**; it's the fastest-acting pain reliever -- besides ibuprofen, I have tried paracetamol, but it doesn't really do anything for the pain. The brand of the ibuprofen matters a lot, as I come to learn (Russian ibuprofen > Bayer's for me).
> - ***FOOD***: This is a tricky one, if you become the biggest picky-eater like me. If you don't want stomach pain alongside your period cramps, you'd better eat something meaty before taking the pill. Please eat what you crave to avoid vomiting later.
> - ***Heater***: Many options of heat pads are available in supermarkets and pharmacies. Portable heating pads are also great, as some can do soft massages.
> - ***Hygiene essentials***: Your sanitary product of choice, an extra pair of everything, will never be an overkill.
> - ***Puke bag***: You never know what will happen.
> - ***Ginger tea***: Fruit brown sugar ginger teas on TOP! Ginger has anti-inflammatory properties, and it can assist your OTC pain relief meds!
> - ***Emergency contact information***: In case of emergencies/ when the pain is too much to handle alone.


I honestly hate how ridiculously fast my metabolism becomes during my period. I am constantly hungry, *craving* that **something** that I can't put my finger on with a foggy brain. Honestly, this is the reason why endometriosis pain ends up being so costly!

Alongside all of this, having a friend(s) around does wonders. They can not only support you physically and emotionally but will distract you from your pain.

> Best of luck!~
