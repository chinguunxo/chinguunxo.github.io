Being a STEM student is fun! You get to understand cool concepts and theories, but you inevitably have to memorize those empirical formulas, amino acids, or functions. Flashcards have been my lifesaver, and I think Anki is the best platform so far. It allows you to review your flashcards effortlessly through active recall, tracks your progress, and you can even gamify it with Xbox controllers! Now I can study even more lying down because my back hurts from studying 8 hours straight.

Here are my tips for high-yield flashcards:

---
# Using AI to generate your flashcards + adding your inputs

I like to use the following prompt:

> Lecture #: Synthesize me a comprehensive Anki TSV file that closely matches the full Lecture # study guide, and use your code writing tool or something to give me something that can work as intended. Be concise and connect concepts.
