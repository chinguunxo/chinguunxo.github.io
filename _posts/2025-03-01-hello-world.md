Every website needs a first post. This is mine.

I've been meaning to set up a personal site for a while — somewhere to write about things I'm building, reading, and thinking about. Not a polished publication. Just a place to think out loud.

## Why bother writing?

Writing forces me to actually understand something. It's easy to feel like you know a topic, but the moment you try to explain it clearly, the gaps appear. Writing is my stress test for ideas.

## What to expect

No fixed schedule, no particular niche. Topics will probably include:

- Things I'm building (code, tools, side projects)
- Books and ideas I've found interesting
- Notes from things I've learned

If any of it is useful to you, great. If not, it's still useful to me.
